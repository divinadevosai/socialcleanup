## Maker's first comment

Post this as soon as the launch goes live:
```
Hey Product Hunt 👋

I built Social Cleanup after realising how much old stuff I'd posted over the years, right as I started job hunting. Recruiters scroll back further than you'd think, and deleting posts one by one is painful.

How it works:
1️⃣ Pick an account (Reddit, X/Twitter or Facebook)
2️⃣ Choose how far back: older than 1, 3 or 5 years, or exact dates
3️⃣ Review everything it finds, keep what you love
4️⃣ It saves a backup to your computer, then deletes the rest in the background

For Instagram, LinkedIn and Threads, which don't allow tools to delete posts safely, it walks you through each site's own tools with a step-by-step checklist instead.

Privacy was the #1 priority:
🔒 Runs entirely in your browser. No servers, no accounts, no tracking
🔑 Never asks for your password
🧹 Erases its list of your posts as soon as it's done
📖 Fully open source, so you can check every line

It's free. I'd love your feedback: which platform should I add next, and what would make it more useful for you?
```

## Launch-day tips

- **Launch after the store listing is approved**, so the main link installs in one click instead of needing developer mode.
- **Schedule for 12:01 AM Pacific**, Tuesday to Thursday. The daily leaderboard resets at midnight PT, so launching early gives you the whole day.
- **Reply to every comment** quickly. Activity in the comments matters more than anything else.
- **Tell people you've launched and ask them to check it out**, but don't ask for upvotes directly. Product Hunt penalises vote requests and vote rings.
- Share the launch on X, LinkedIn and Instagram with the promo video and a link to the Product Hunt page.
- Be ready for questions about terms of service and safety. The honest answer: it only acts on your own account, at a steady pace, after you confirm, with a backup first; and where sites don't allow tools, it guides you instead.
